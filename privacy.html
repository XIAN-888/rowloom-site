Rowloom — Privacy

Effective: November 12, 2026 · Updated: November 14, 2026

Rowloom keeps your imported patterns and knitting records on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your pattern files, project names, notes or row history.

Local project information

The app stores imported pattern copies, project and piece names, completed rows, reading and ruler positions, repeat rules, row reminders, execution plans and their saved source positions, action confirmation records, named checkpoint snapshots, notes and recent undo history locally. Images may be converted or optimized on the device for reading. No app login, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking identifier or AI analysis service is used.

Apple purchases

Apple processes StoreKit purchases and subscriptions. The app uses purchase entitlement information to determine whether more personal projects may be added. Your knitting files are not sent to us for a purchase. Apple’s handling of account and payment information is described in Apple.

Backups and destinations you choose

Backup export packages your patterns and progress into a file. You choose where it goes using Files, which may include iCloud Drive or another provider. That destination handles the exported copy under its own terms. Rowloom does not automatically synchronize projects through a developer service. Apple device backup settings are separate from this app’s manual backup feature.

Deleting and retaining local data

Delete a project in the app to remove its local project record. Export a backup first if you want to keep it. Saved backup copies remain wherever you placed them and must be managed in Files or with that provider. Removing the app is subject to the operating system’s app-data and backup behavior; it does not remove independently exported copies.

Support email and this website

If you email xiangguoxin520@icloud.com, we receive your email address and what you choose to include and use them to respond. Please send only what is needed. This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts or advertising trackers; GitHub’s handling of website requests is described in GitHub.

Questions and updates

Contact xiangguoxin520@icloud.com for privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence. This page describes the current app and support site. If their data practices change, this policy will be updated.
