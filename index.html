Plan the row. Check every action. Resume with context.

Turn your own PDF or image pattern into a knitting route you enter and review. Keep its stages, simultaneous row actions and saved working states together in Rowloom.

Plan, check & resume knitting

  • Build an execution plan for your own pattern: enter stages, actual starting rows, pattern rows and repeats, then save a source position for each stage.
  • Rehearse any actual row in the plan draft. See its stage, pattern row, repeat, every action due and saved source page without changing your knitting progress.
  • Check each action before finishing a row. When a decrease and a color change coincide, both need their own check; a changed plan does not silently reuse the current row's earlier checks.
  • Name a checkpoint that keeps progress, plan, rules, action checks, note and pattern position beyond recent undo history. Preview what will change before restoring the complete state; you can also undo the restoration.
  • Read PDFs and images on iPhone and iPad, with separate progress and notes for each piece. Manual reading and counting remain available without a plan. You position the ruler yourself; it does not follow the row count automatically.
  • Try a separate original 20-stitch, 20-row practice. Its prepared example has two actions on actual row 11 and a checkpoint after 8 completed rows; it does not change your projects.
  • Export a full backup, review its contents, and restore independent copies alongside existing projects. Action checks record your decisions; they do not inspect the fabric or perform physical knitting.

Start with three personal projects

Keep up to three of your own projects for free. Practice samples do not count toward this allowance. All core knitting tools in existing projects, backup export and backup restoration remain available without Pro.

More projects with Pro

An optional yearly subscription or one-time Lifetime purchase lets you add more personal projects. Existing projects stay usable after a yearly subscription expires; expiry only affects adding new imports when you are at the free allowance. File, storage and reasonable safety limits still apply.

The yearly subscription renews automatically until canceled. Current local prices and billing terms appear before purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. A Lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing yearly subscription. Restore purchases from the Pro screen using the same Apple Account.

Privacy

Your pattern files and progress are stored on your device. No app account is required. Rowloom does not automatically sync projects. You can choose Files, including iCloud Drive, as a backup destination and restore a saved backup on another device.

Pattern rights and help

Please import only patterns you own or have permission to use. Importing a pattern does not grant permission to redistribute the designer’s work.
