Your pattern. Your place. Your next row.

Keep your knitting pattern and your place together. Rowloom is a calm companion for reading PDF or image patterns and recording progress, one row at a time.

Pattern reader & row counter

  • Import a PDF or image from Files and read your own pattern on iPhone or iPad.
  • Place a manual ruler on the pattern. Adjust it yourself as you work; counting does not move it automatically.
  • Record completed rows, see the current pattern row and repeat, and display reminders at the rows you choose.
  • Organise a project into pieces, each with its own progress, reading position, repeat rules and note.
  • Undo recent changes, rewind to an earlier row, or mark a project finished and resume it later.
  • Export a full backup of patterns and progress, then review a backup before restoring it as copies alongside existing projects.

Start with three personal projects

Keep up to three of your own projects for free. Practice samples do not count toward this allowance. All core knitting tools in existing projects, backup export and backup restoration remain available without Pro.

More projects with Pro

An optional yearly subscription or one-time Lifetime purchase lets you add more personal projects. Existing projects stay usable after a yearly subscription expires; expiry only affects adding new imports when you are at the free allowance. File, storage and reasonable safety limits still apply.

The yearly subscription renews automatically until cancelled. Current local prices and billing terms appear before purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. A Lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing yearly subscription. Restore purchases from the Pro screen using the same Apple Account.

Privacy

Your pattern files and progress are stored on your device. No app account is required. Rowloom does not automatically sync projects. You can choose Files, including iCloud Drive, as a backup destination and restore a saved backup on another device.

Pattern rights and help

Please import only patterns you own or have permission to use. Importing a pattern does not grant permission to redistribute the designer’s work.
